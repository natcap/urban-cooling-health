Why the Green–Target cooling ranking changed

11 November 2026 · HTML · Figure 3 review and figures

Finding in brief

The Green30 reversal is not explained simply by moving canopy or upgrading InVEST. A controlled, already-existing NoData-only test is the largest isolated driver of the Green30 cooling reduction. Revised canopy placement partly offsets that reduction, while the revised Target design adds eligible canopy and increases Target cooling. The precise decomposition is verified for the 30% tier; equivalent NoData-only controls for 10% and 20% were not found or run.

This is a read-only analysis of existing model outputs and current input files. No model reruns, Word changes or pushes were performed. Numbers below use one common London footprint, not the population-valid mask used in older diagnostic reports. Confidence: share with the stated attribution limits.

1. Isolating Green30: a controlled sequence

All rows use the same 15,733,893 London cells and 33 boroughs; cooling is shown as a positive reduction in temperature. The audit mask excludes 63 cells that are present in the production Figure 3 mask so every historical/diagnostic raster has identical support. No resampling was needed.

  • NoData-only effect: reduces cooling by 0.049786°C in borough means and 0.076973°C in the London-area-weighted mean.
  • Subsequent canopy-scenario effect: restores 0.012330°C and 0.003454°C, respectively. This includes changed planting positions, class composition and removal of woodland-to-100 relabelling; those individual contributions were not separately modelled.
  • Net Green30 reduction: 0.037456°C across borough means and 0.073519°C area-weighted. A single percentage attribution would mislead because the canopy change offsets part of the NoData effect.

The NoData-only step leaves the valid land-cover classes and their spatial mask identical, verified directly. It holds software versions, all non-LULC arguments and ancillary input checksums constant. This is a numerical missing-data/convolution sensitivity, not a physical reduction in tree canopy. Its exact internal numerical pathway is not fully diagnosed here. It is not the same as merely excluding 63 cells during summary calculation: the output temperatures differ on the very same valid London cells.

For comparison, historical Target30 cooling is 0.366814°C across boroughs (0.310221°C area-weighted). Even holding that Target result unchanged, the NoData-harmonized historical Green30 falls below it. Thus the existing controlled test is sufficient to reverse the displayed 30% ranking before the new canopy scenario is introduced. This does not establish an exact version-only effect for historical Target30, for which an unchanged-input control was not available.

Expressed directly as Target30 cooling minus Green30 cooling, the borough ranking shifts from −0.007022°C (Green advantage) to +0.041586°C (Target advantage). The 0.048608°C shift decomposes along this sequence into about +0.049786°C from Green NoData harmonization, −0.012330°C from the subsequent Green canopy redesign, and +0.011152°C from the combined Target revision, with a negligible historical-versus-rerun Green residual. This is a sequential decomposition, not a factorial estimate of independent effects or interactions.

Green: eligible canopy quantity is unchanged, but placement is not. The revised generator uses focal codes {1,2,100} instead of {1,2,4,20,21}, restricts conversions to {4,20,21}, and builds nested increments. Historical Green also changed 12,232 / 24,916 / 35,751 woodland cells to code 100; those changes are removed. They were not newly added canopy, but were not necessarily thermally neutral: the biophysical table assigns woodland Kc=0.7 and code100 Kc=0.8. Revised Green30 shifts eligible additions from grassland toward suburban and urban classes (full transition counts in the audit CSV).

Target: every historically eligible added cell is retained; additional eligible cells fill the shortfall to each Green budget. Revised scenarios also exclude 3,188 / 7,248 / 11,323 old conversions from ineligible classes. Selection uses deterministic (rank, FID) ordering and a 5 m radius with pixel-centre inclusion. The eligible area increments are 3.4526, 5.9347 and 3.6128 km². The old Green–Target comparison therefore did not match eligible added area.

Revised Target additions are 71.3%, 69.1% and 67.3% Urban (code20), versus 23.6%, 25.0% and 27.1% for Green. Converting low-shade, low-Kc urban surfaces to tree cover can yield more additional cooling than replacing already-green land. This is a mechanistic explanation consistent with the input table, not a separately identified causal contribution. Candidate-domain and source-class differences mean the benefit cannot be attributed solely to the social-vulnerability ranking. Full scenario rules remain in the scenario README, not duplicated here.

3. Model-version and grid effects: what is and is not established

  • Grid geometry: all 17 temperature rasters checked share the same 5,840×4,509 grid, 10 m cells, origin (503560, 200940), and projected CRS. The ranking reversal remains on an identical London mask. A change of output grid origin or aggregation footprint is not needed to produce it.
  • Historical Green30/baseline versus existing 3.20.2 reruns: paired cooling differs by about 10⁻⁸°C in these summaries, effectively zero. A script header saying “generated by 3.14.1” does not identify the runtime that produced the archived Figure 3 raster; do not label this a verified 3.14.1→3.20.2 experiment.
  • The earlier +0.438°C diagnostic is a different comparison: it tested an intermediate Target30-v4 input across model formulas/grids. It concerns absolute Target temperature, not the final old-versus-revised paired Green–Target cooling contrast. Its +0.501°C park-formula and −0.063°C alignment contributions must not be imported into this decomposition. See the version investigation.
  • Target: the old-to-new changes combine revised area/eligibility/placement with any unverified historical runtime effects. Without a same-input Target rerun, a numerical percentage assigned to model version would be speculative. Historical Target filenames follow the confirmed manuscript aliases, but an immutable original-run input checksum/runtime record was not established here; current-file transition counts are not proof of every historical run snapshot.
  • 10/20 tiers: both old Green inputs use NoData255 and revised inputs use NoData0. Their temperature shifts are consistent with the 30% diagnosis, but its numerical attribution cannot be transferred to them without controls.

4. Meaning for the manuscript and next decision

Use the internally consistent revised outputs. Describe Target benefits as results of the revised matched-area allocation design, not proof that vulnerability ranking alone improves cooling. Keep the prescribed-climate caveat from the Figure 3 review. The old comparison is useful provenance, not an alternative set of numbers to combine with revised health outcomes.

For strict attribution at every tier, the smallest next experiment is to rerun historical Green10/20 with only NoData changed, plus historical Target10/20/30 unchanged in 3.20.2. To separate individual canopy-rule effects further would require additional counterfactual LULC runs. None were launched in this audit. The NoData sensitivity also warrants a focused model-input handling test before claiming absolute numerical robustness; it should not be hidden as a cosmetic file-format change.
