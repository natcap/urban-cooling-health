# ONS enquiry — draft only, not sent

To: Health.Data@ons.gov.uk  
Subject: Availability of 2021 seasonal cause-specific deaths for London boroughs

Dear ONS Health Data team,

For a study of urban cooling and health in London, could you advise whether you can provide 2021 death counts for all 33 London boroughs (E09000001–E09000033), by area of usual residence, both sexes combined and all ages?

We need monthly counts, or June–August totals if monthly data are unavailable, for these underlying causes:

- All causes
- Cardiovascular diseases: I00–I99
- Respiratory diseases: J00–J99
- Mental and behavioural disorders: F00–F99
- Intentional self-harm: X60–X84

Month of death occurrence would be preferable; please let us know if only registration-based counts are available. Annual totals for reconciliation, the extraction date, and any suppression or disclosure-control notes would also be helpful.

Is an existing public dataset available, or would this require a bespoke extract? Please advise on availability, estimated cost and turnaround time, including whether summer totals would be quicker to provide.

Many thanks,
Yingjie Li
